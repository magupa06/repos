Kebab Amigo

Ingredients

Chicken

Lettuce

Tortilla

Greek Sauce

Carrots

Steps to follow:

  1. Cut and cook your chicken
  2. While the chicken is cooking. Peal, cut and fry your potatoes
  3. Chop your lettuce while everything else is cooking.
  4. When everything is close to done, you heat the tortillas.
  5. Put all together and enjoy your kebab amigo!
