Kebab Amigo

Ingredients

  1. Chicken
  2. Tortillas
  3. Potatoes
  4. Lettuce
  5. Greek sauce

Steps to follow:

  • Cut and cook your chicken
  • While the chicken is cooking. Peal, cut and fry your potatoes
  • Chop your lettuce while everything else is cooking.
  • When everything is close to done, you heat the tortillas.
  • Put all together and enjoy your kebab amigo!
