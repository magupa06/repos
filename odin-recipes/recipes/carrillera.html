Carrillera Recipe

Steps to follow:

  • Season the beef cheeks with salt and pepper and sear them on high heat until browned on all sides.
  • While the meat sears, chop the onion, garlic, carrot and celery.
  • Remove the beef cheeks and saute the vegetables in the same pot until soft.
  • Return the beef cheeks to the pot, pour in the red wine and beef broth, and bring to a simmer.
  • Cover and braise on low heat for about 3 hours, until the meat is fork-tender. Serve with mashed potatoes or rice and enjoy!

Ingredients

  1. Beef cheek (carrillera)
  2. Red wine
  3. Carrot and celery
  4. Onion and garlic
  5. Beef broth
