Lasagna Recipe

Ingredients

Beef

Tomato Sauce

Pasta

Eggplant

Cheese

Steps to follow:

  1. Soften the pasta
  2. Cook the meat in low heat while cutting the eggplant.
  3. Make your own tomato sauce by cutting and adding the tomatoes into a pod. You could also buy the tomato sauce in the supermaket.
  4. Put the beef, eggplant and tomato sauce between the pasta layers.
  5. Finish up with adding more tomato sauce and cheese on top. Put in the oven for 30min to make sure the cheese is grilled enough and enjoy!
