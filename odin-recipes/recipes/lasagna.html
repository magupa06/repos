Lasagna Recipe

Ingredients

  1. Beef
  2. Tomato Sauce
  3. Pasta
  4. Eggplant
  5. Cheese

Steps to follow:

  • Soften the pasta
  • Cook the meat in low heat while cutting the eggplant.
    •
  • Make your own tomato sauce by cutting and adding the tomatoes into a pod. You could also buy the tomato sauce in the supermaket.
  • Put the beef, eggplant and tomato sauce between the pasta layers.
  • Finish up with adding more tomato sauce and cheese on top. Put in the oven for 30min to make sure the cheese is grilled enough and enjoy!
