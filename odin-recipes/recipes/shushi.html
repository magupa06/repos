Shushi Recipe

Ingredients

Sushi rice

Nori sheets

Salmon

Rice vinegar

Soy sauce

Steps to follow:

  1. Cook the sushi rice and season it with rice vinegar while it cools down.
  2. While the rice cools, slice the salmon into thin, even strips.
  3. Lay a nori sheet on a bamboo mat and spread a thin layer of rice on top.
  4. Place the salmon strips on the rice and roll everything tightly with the mat.
  5. Slice the roll into pieces and serve with soy sauce. Enjoy your shushi!
