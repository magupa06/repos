Shushi Recipe

Ingredients

  1. Sushi rice
  2. Nori sheets
  3. Salmon
  4. Rice vinegar
  5. Soy sauce

Steps to follow:

  • Cook the sushi rice and season it with rice vinegar while it cools down.
  • While the rice cools, slice the salmon into thin, even strips.
  • Lay a nori sheet on a bamboo mat and spread a thin layer of rice on top.
  • Place the salmon strips on the rice and roll everything tightly with the mat.
  • Slice the roll into pieces and serve with soy sauce. Enjoy your shushi!
